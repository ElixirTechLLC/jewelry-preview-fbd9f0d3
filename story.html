Wolf’s Fine Jewelry is built on a simple belief: exceptional jewelry creates lasting meaning. Everything here is designed, cast, set and finished by us, in Tappan, New York.

Our approach

The Wolf’s Philosophy

Most jewelers sell pieces made somewhere else. We make ours. The same people who draw a ring cast it, set its stones and polish it, which is why we can list the gold weight and diamond weight of every piece, and why we can change almost anything about it for you.

Craftsmanship

Made to Be Treasured

We work in 10K, 14K and 18K gold, yellow, white and rose. Chains are solid, never hollow. Every stone is set under a microscope and every piece is weighed before it leaves the bench. Bring it back any time for cleaning and a check of the settings.
