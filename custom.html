The process

From an idea to something you can hold.

  1. ConsultationIn the shop, by phone or by email. Show us a photo, a sketch, an heirloom, or a piece from this site you want changed. We talk design, stones and budget.
  2. Design & renderingWe model your piece in 3D and send photorealistic renderings — with the exact gold weight, diamond weight and price — and revise until you approve.
  3. CastingYour design is cast in solid 10K, 14K or 18K gold — yellow, white or rose.
  4. Setting & finishingEvery stone is set by hand under a microscope, then the piece is polished, inspected and weighed.
  5. YoursCollect it at the shop or have it shipped. Financing is available on every custom piece.
