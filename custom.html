Custom design

We design, cast and set jewelry in our own shop, so we can make almost anything: rings, chains, pendants, bracelets and diamond work on watches.

Start a design

How a custom piece is made

  1. ConsultationIn the shop, by phone or by email. Bring a photo, a sketch, an heirloom, or a piece from this site you want changed. We talk through design, stones and budget.
  2. Design and renderingWe model the piece in 3D and send you renderings with the gold weight, diamond weight and price. We revise until you approve it.
  3. CastingThe piece is cast in solid 10K, 14K or 18K gold, in yellow, white or rose.
  4. Setting and finishingStones are set by hand under a microscope. The piece is then polished, inspected and weighed.
  5. Pickup or deliveryCollect it at the shop or have it shipped. Financing is available on custom orders.

What we make

  • Engagement rings and wedding bandsAny shape and setting. Bring your own stone or we can source one. Bands shaped to fit your ring.
  • Pendants and name piecesFrom a logo, a photo or a drawing. Nameplates and initials.
  • Chains and braceletsCuban, rope, Franco, tennis and others, in the length and width you want.
  • Men's ringsSignets, clusters and full pavé rings like The Sovereign.
  • WatchesDiamond bezels, dials and bracelets set in our shop.
  • RedesignsOld gold and inherited stones remade into a new piece.
  • Changes to a piece on this siteA different size, gold color, karat, stone shape or carat weight.
